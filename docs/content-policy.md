# Content Policy

## Public Baseline

Approved public framing for v1:

- SEDA: Software for Evolving Digital Art.
- Research, software, and infrastructure for evolving digital art.
- Focus on preserving network-interactive and evolving digital art.
- ARKIVO.ART is the current public alpha project.
- The SEDA Nodes post-doctoral project may be mentioned directly, using publicly known details and any project details approved by Dr. Farinha.
- Public links may include `https://arkivo.art`, `https://github.com/seda-studio`, and the public thesis DOI.

## Do Not Publish Yet

- Operational SEDA node details.
- Node email accounts, hostnames, IPs, tunnels, WiFi, passwords, or scripts from private setup notes.
- Unconfirmed post-doc funding, timelines, research-unit claims, or details not approved for publication.
- Teia partnership or infrastructure commitments.
- CONNKT product claims until there is a reviewed public release story.
- Volunteer node signup promises.

## Tone

Use clear, precise language. Prefer “explores”, “research”, “alpha”, and “active development” where those terms are accurate; avoid claims of completed infrastructure unless the claim is approved and verifiable.

## Approval Rule

If a claim depends on internal research notes rather than an existing public artifact, do not publish it without explicit approval.
