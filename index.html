SEDA

Software for Evolving Digital Art.

SEDA explores tools and practices for preserving network-interactive and evolving digital art, with a focus on Tezos-based crypto-art and the long-term care of works whose behavior depends on code, APIs, storage, and network context.

SEDA brings together research, software, and infrastructure work for the preservation of evolving digital art.

Projects

ARKIVO.ART

ARKIVO is an alpha preservation platform for network-interactive and evolving crypto-art. It catalogs selected Tezos OBJKTs, captures snapshots, and documents artwork behavior for future conservation work.
